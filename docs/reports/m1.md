## Decisions made without asking
- Added six weaknesses beyond the ten from the earlier list
  (long-context-degradation, hallucination, reversal-curse, sycophancy,
  prompt-injection, judge-position-bias) because they are the most-cited
  weaknesses in the literature and they demonstrate the taxonomy's breadth.
- Five techniques are marked `proposed` because they are practitioner
  practice without a controlled study: checklist-decomposition,
  irreversible-action-gate, reread-before-edit, dependency-existence-check,
  and context-hygiene's parent linkage. They are labeled as such on the site.
- Claim ids use single hyphens throughout (the spec's original `--`
  separator is not a valid slug).
- arXiv rate-limits this machine hard after a handful of requests, so CI
  runs paper verification only on push to main, not on every PR.
- `.claude/launch.json` added so `next dev` can be launched by tooling.
